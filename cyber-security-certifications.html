Important

Certification Preparation vs Official Certification

SKILL IT Education provides training and certification preparation for selected industry-recognized credentials. Certification exams are administered by their respective organizations. SKILL IT Education does not imply official affiliation, authorization or endorsement unless explicitly stated.

SKILL IT Education provides training and preparation for these credentials. The certification exam itself is scheduled, administered and issued by the respective organization — not by SKILL IT Education.
